Privacy Policy for Motion Cricket for Android TV

Developer: AIO TECH
Effective date: 5 November 2026

Motion Cricket for Android TV ("the app") is developed by AIO TECH. It turns your phone into a motion-controlled cricket bat for a game on your Android TV.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not send data to any server.

How the app uses your device

  • Motion sensors (phone): the gyroscope, accelerometer and orientation sensors detect your swings. These readings are used only while you are batting or using Practice Nets.
  • Local Wi-Fi network: when you tap Connect to TV, your phone sends swing data directly to the app on your own TV over your local Wi-Fi network. This data stays on your network, is used only to play the game, and is not kept after use.
  • Volume buttons (phone): while connected, the app reads volume button presses to move your batter, and restores your media volume when you stop.
  • Settings: preferences such as batting hand and your best Practice Nets score are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Permissions

The internet and network permissions are used only for the local connection between your phone and TV. The foreground service and notification permissions keep the bat connected while your phone is locked, and show a notification with a Stop button while it is active.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
